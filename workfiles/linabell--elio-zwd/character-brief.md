# Character Brief

## Canonical identity

- English name: **LinaBell**
- Chinese name: **玲娜贝儿**
- Character type: pink fox character associated with Duffy and Friends
- Planned repository category: `Mascots`
- Planned canonical key: `fictional/disney/duffy-and-friends/linabell`
- Official reference page: `https://www.shanghaidisneyresort.com/zh-cn/experience?group=characters`

## Character read at desktop-pet scale

The pet must remain recognizable at a `192x208` cell size. Preserve these anchors in every frame:

1. oversized upright triangular ears with pale inner fur;
2. warm pink plush fur;
3. large cream-white eye patches and muzzle;
4. large saturated blue eyes with clear lashes;
5. small soft-pink nose and cheerful mouth;
6. large fluffy tail with a pale tip or underside;
7. compact rounded body with plush-toy proportions;
8. curious, observant and energetic personality.

## Main outfit — pink spring variant

Use the two pink-costume references as the primary outfit family:

- soft pink sun hat with a pale brim;
- one large flower or bow accent placed to the character's left;
- pink and pale-yellow checked top or dress;
- green neck bow;
- cherry motif simplified into a small readable emblem;
- pink skirt with a broad, clean silhouette.

Small fabric patterns may be simplified. The hat shape, green neck bow and cherry motif should remain large enough to read at pet scale.

## Secondary outfit — blue sailor variant

The blue sailor costume is useful for full-body proportion and tail reference, but it should not be mixed into the main atlas. Treat it as a later independent variant so animation rows remain visually coherent.

## Personality and action language

The visual acting should emphasize:

- curiosity: head tilts, attentive ears and directional looking;
- friendliness: broad paw wave and open smile;
- cleverness: magnifying-glass or clipboard review pose;
- energy: springy running and jumping;
- softness: gentle idle breathing, blinking and tail sway;
- resilience: a cute disappointed state that recovers cleanly.

## Props

Preferred props, used sparingly and only when attached to the character silhouette:

- small magnifying glass for detective/review actions;
- compact clipboard for waiting/review actions;
- optional small flower detail matching the hat.

Avoid readable writing, UI panels, floating icons, logos, detached sparkles, floor shadows and scenery.

## Style target

- Style preset: `plush` with chibi simplification
- Material: soft short plush, not photoreal mascot photography
- Rendering: clean, bright, consistent lighting
- Silhouette: whole body visible, centered, no cropped ears or tail
- Background: removable flat chroma background during production, transparent in final atlas
- Line/details: broad and simple enough to survive downscaling

## Identity failure conditions

Reject or repair frames when any of these occur:

- ears become rounded, drooped by default or vary greatly in size;
- eyes change from blue or lose the characteristic large eye patches;
- muzzle shape, nose position or facial proportions drift;
- tail disappears, changes species or changes size between rows;
- pink outfit elements change color or migrate unpredictably;
- the character becomes a generic pink cat instead of a fox-like LinaBell silhouette;
- props replace the character's pose rather than support the action;
- official image pixels, logos or readable branded text appear in the final art.
